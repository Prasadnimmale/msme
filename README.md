# SME Growth Circle – MSME Business Visibility & Growth Platform

Official static multi-page Next.js (App Router) website for **SME Growth Circle** (an initiative of **SME Growth Circle Foundation**).

## Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router) with Static Export (`output: 'export'`)
- **Library:** React 19
- **Icons:** Lucide React (`lucide-react`)
- **Styling:** Custom Responsive CSS3 (`src/app/globals.css`)
- **Typography:** Google Sans (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700)

## Website Pages

1. **Home (`/`)** – Hero banner, About introduction, Why Choose SME Growth Circle, Our 5 Pillars of Growth, Our Solutions, How It Works, Who Can Join, Vision & Mission, and CTA banner.
2. **About Us (`/about`)** – About SME Growth Circle, Our Story, Vision, Mission, Objectives, Core Values, Commitment to MSMEs, and Impact on Andhra Pradesh MSMEs.
3. **Our Solutions (`/solutions`)** – Business Visibility, Market Access, Digital Marketplace, Business Networking, Buyer-Seller Connect, Business Development, Learning & Training, Government Support, and Events & Industry Connect.
4. **Marketplace (`/marketplace`)** – Static product & service search UI, 7 sector categories (Manufacturing, Agriculture, Food & Beverages, Handlooms & Textiles, Engineering & Machinery, Chemicals & Plastics, Home & Lifestyle), and Featured MSME Products.
5. **Membership (`/membership`)** – Why Become a Member, Membership Benefits, Who Can Join, Business Networking Benefits, Membership CTA, and Static Membership Enquiry Form UI.
6. **Events (`/events`)** – Business Conclaves, Exhibitions, Trade Shows, Buyer-Seller Meets, Networking Events, and Industry Interactions.
7. **Contact Us (`/contact`)** – Editable contact information placeholders (Office Address, Email, Phone, Working Hours), Static Contact Enquiry Form UI, and Google Maps visual placeholder.

## Commands

```bash
# Run development server
npm run dev

# Build static production export (generates /out folder)
npm run build
```
